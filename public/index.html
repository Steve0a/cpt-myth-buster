August 2026 CPT update

What SEVP changed, and what to do about it.

On August 12 and August 24, 2026, SEVP (the DHS program that runs CPT) sent guidance to every school's DSOs. The main points:

  • CPT is only for practical training that is required of every student in your curriculum, U.S. citizens included.
  • Your degree must depend on completing it. If there's another way to finish, CPT isn't available.
  • Elective internships and course credit alone no longer qualify.
  • Past approvals for similar students don't guarantee future approvals.
  • Schools that don't comply risk losing SEVP certification, so many are reviewing or pausing CPT.

What to do: ask your academic department and DSO whether practical training is required for everyone in your program. If not, ask about OPT.

“In contrast, CPT fulfills an integral element of an established curriculum and successful completion is contingent on participation in CPT.”

How CPT works

Most schools follow this order. Your International Student office may add its own forms or deadlines.

Check eligibility

One full academic year in F-1 status (some graduate programs are exempt), and since Aug 2026, training required of every student in your program.

Apply to jobs

Apply and interview now. You do not need CPT approval to apply or interview.

Get a written offer

It should list job title, duties related to your major, start and end dates, hours per week, location and supervisor.

Enroll and get approval

Register for the required internship or practicum course and get sign-off from your academic advisor, if your school requires it.

DSO authorizes CPT

Your DSO (school visa advisor) records it in SEVIS and gives you a new I-20 showing the employer, dates and full- or part-time.

Start on the approved date

Not a day earlier. Show your employer the updated I-20 for Form I-9. Changes need a new authorization.

CPT vs. OPT

They're often confused, including by recruiters.

CPTOPT
WhenWhile you are still studying, before program completionMostly after you graduate (pre-completion OPT also exists)
Who approvesYour school's DSOUSCIS, after your DSO recommends it
Application to USCISNo. No EAD card, no USCIS feeYes. Form I-765, fee, EAD card
Job offer needed firstYes. CPT is tied to one employerNo for standard OPT. You can apply while job searching
Tied to curriculumYes. Since Aug 2026, must be required of every student in your programMust relate to your field of study
Time limitNo cap, but 1 year+ of full-time CPT ends post-completion OPT eligibility12 months, plus a 24-month STEM extension if eligible
Is it "sponsorship"?No. Neither CPT nor OPT requires an employer to sponsor a visa. H-1B is sponsorship.
